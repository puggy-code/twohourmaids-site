Quick answers

Who comes to my home?

A team of two of our own employees, trained by us. Every new hire gets a background check, and we are insured.

What do you use to clean and sanitize?

Each team brings its own kit, with fresh rags, gloves and masks for every home. We disinfect the places hands touch. We bring our own vacuum.

How long does it take, and what gets done?

The Two Hour Clean is two people for two hours: the kitchen, bathrooms and floors. Want a full checklist done? Pick the Checklist Clean.

Can I add extras to a Two Hour Clean?

No extras. Tell us the one or two things that matter most, and the team starts there. For every room, pick the Checklist Clean.
